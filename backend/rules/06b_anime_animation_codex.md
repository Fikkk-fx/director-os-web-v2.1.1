## 🏛️ THE 8 ZERO-3D ANIME PRODUCTION LAWS (HUKUM MUTLAK 2D SAKUGA V20.5)

Untuk proyek **Pure 2D Hand-Drawn Anime**, sistem **MENGHARAMKAN 100% KATA 3D, CGI, DAN PARAMETER OPTIK LIVE-ACTION** (*The Hermetic Genre Air-Gap Mandate*):

1. **Law 1 — Pure 2D Hand-Drawn Layout:** Wajib `"Pure traditional 2D hand-drawn anime layout, crisp G-pen ink contours, 0% 3D models"`.
2. **Law 2 — Variable Koma-Ochi Cadence & Obake Smear Modulation:** Modulasi dinamis ketukan frame rate animasi: *San-koma (8fps)* pada keheningan kontemplatif $\rightarrow$ *Ni-koma (12fps)* pada langkah kaki & dialog $\rightarrow$ meledak ke *Ichi-koma (24fps)* pada puncak benturan Sakuga dengan distorsi kuas goresan *Obake Smear Frames*.
3. **Law 3 — 2-Tone Nido-Kage Cel Shading:** Bayangan karakter terdiri dari maksimal 2–3 tingkat warna flat (*flat base color + sharp dark shadow zone + subtle rim highlight*).
4. **Law 4 — Nicker Poster Color Painted Backgrounds:** Latar belakang dilukis dengan gaya cat poster Nicker bertekstur kuas alami.
5. **Law 5 — Inverted Monochrome Impact Frames:** Momen benturan fisik keras memicu kilatan 1-2 frame hitam-putih terbalik (*monochrome impact frames*).
6. **Law 6 — Dynamic Speed Lines & Nakamura Debris:** Debu dan serpihan ledakan berbentuk balok kubus segitiga geometris berputar (*Nakamura block debris*).
7. **Law 7 — Hand-Drawn Lens Flare & Watery Tear Shimmer:** Pembiasan cahaya digambar dengan tangan berupa lingkaran oranye-cyan dan kemilau air mata 12fps.
8. **Law 8 — Absolute Zero 3D/CGI Lexicon Quarantine:** BANNED TOKENS: `Unreal Engine`, `Ray-Tracing`, `SSS Pores`, `Photorealistic`, `3D render`, `Blender`, `Octane`.

## 🌀 THE VARIABLE KOMA-OCHI FRAME MODULATION & OBAKE SMEAR ENGINE

Animasi 2D Jepang terbaik (Ufotable, Studio Trigger, KyoAni, Ghibli) tidak pernah digambar pada satu kecepatan frame konstan. Sistem wajib menerapkan modulasi ritme 3-tahap:
1. **San-Koma (8fps / Shooting on Threes):** Digunakan saat dialog hening, tatapan mata emosional mendalam, gemersik daun sepoi-sepoi, atau gestur mikro intim (memberikan ruang bernapas puitis).
2. **Ni-Koma (12fps / Shooting on Twos):** Standar ritme anime televisi klasik untuk langkah berjalan, percakapan normal, dan aksi standar.
3. **Ichi-Koma (24fps / Shooting on Ones) & Obake Smear Frames:** Puncak Sakuga klimaks, sabetan pedang, ledakan elemen energi api/petir, dan akrobat ekstrem di mana setiap frame digambar unik (*full 24 discrete drawings per second*), dipadukan dengan *Obake* (peregangan anatomi kuas manual elastis) sebelum terkunci kaku pada pose impak (*impact frame lock*).

# 🌸 MASTER ANIME & ANIMATION DIRECTORS

### 1. HAYAO MIYAZAKI & STUDIO GHIBLI (*Spirited Away, Princess Mononoke, Howl's Moving Castle*)
- **Filsafat Animasi:** *Sakuga Fluidity, Environmental Breath & Flying Dynamics (Mono no Aware)*.
- **Visual:** Rumput hijau zamrud berombak tertiup angin, langit biru berawan kumulus putih raksasa, dan partikel debu peri melayang berkilau dalam sinar matahari lembut.
- **Prompt Anchor:** `"Studio Ghibli animation aesthetic by Hayao Miyazaki, lush hand-drawn 2D Sakuga layout, vibrant emerald grass swaying in wind, towering cumulus clouds in brilliant blue sky, soft painterly sunlight, whimsical nature spirit realism"`.

### 2. MAKOTO SHINKAI & COMIX WAVE FILMS (*Your Name, Weathering With You, Suzume*)
- **Filsafat Visual:** *Hyper-Detailed Atmospheric Lighting, Twilight Magic Hour & Lens Flares*.
- **Visual:** Kilau tetesan hujan di aspal kaca, pantulan langit senja emas-ungu (*twilight magic hour 5600K-3200K*), pembiasan cahaya pelangi (*rainbow optical flares*), dan tetesan air mata ultra-slow-motion.
- **Prompt Anchor:** `"Makoto Shinkai anime cinematography by Comix Wave Films, hyper-detailed 2D lighting, radiant twilight purple and golden hour sky, rainbow lens flares, reflective rain puddles on Tokyo asphalt, emotional tearjerker atmosphere"`.

### 3. SATOSHI KON & MADHOUSE (*Perfect Blue, Paprika, Millennium Actress*)
- **Filsafat Editing:** *Graphic Match-Cut Reality Warping & Psychological Surrealism*.
- **Mekanika Transisi:** Memotong antar-ruang menggunakan bentuk objek identik (*graphic match-cut*), membaurkan batas antara delusi dan kenyataan perkotaan neon.
- **Prompt Anchor:** `"Anime cinematography in Satoshi Kon style, seamless graphic match-cut transitions, surreal psychological reality warping, vivid saturated neon urban lighting, precise anime linework"`.

### 4. HIDEAKI ANNO & STUDIO KHARA (*Neon Genesis Evangelion, Shin Godzilla*)
- **Filsafat Kamera:** *Extreme Monolithic Low-Angle, Industrial Geometry & Apocalyptic Horizon*.
- **Komposisi:** Kamera di aspal membingkai raksasa humanoid menjulang tinggi menutupi langit merah darah di balik tiang listrik dan kabel beton simetris.
- **Prompt Anchor:** `"Cinematography in Hideaki Anno style, extreme ground-level worm's eye view, monolithic scale framing against towering power lines and concrete urban geometry, apocalyptic blood-red horizon"`.

### 5. HARUO SOTOZAKI & UFOTABLE (*Demon Slayer, Fate/stay night: Heaven's Feel*)

- **Visual:** Kamera meluncur menembus salju/hutan, sabetan pedang 2D berpartikel api/air/petir pendaran tinggi (*high-luminance solar flame particles*).
- **Prompt Anchor:** `"Cinematography by Haruo Sotozaki and Ufotable, dynamic camera projection tracking through dense forest, hyper-fluid 2D sakuga sword slashes with blazing solar flame particles, deep cinematic contrast"`.

### 6. NAOKO YAMADA & KYOTO ANIMATION (*A Silent Voice / Koe no Katachi, Liz and the Blue Bird*)
- **Filsafat Kamera:** *Shallow Depth-of-Field Macro Lens, Micro-Body Language & Pastel Intimacy*.

- **Prompt Anchor:** `"Kyoto Animation cinematography directed by Naoko Yamada, shallow depth of field 50mm f/1.2 aesthetic, delicate micro-acting focusing on trembling fingers and shoes, soft pastel watercolor grading"`.
